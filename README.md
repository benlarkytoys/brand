## About Benlarky

Benlarky makes wooden puzzles and model kits for curious, creative adults. Pick a world. Take your time. Make it yours.

Our two product directions are **wooden puzzles** and **3D wooden model kits**.

### Our mission

We design hands-on experiences that respect your time and reward it with something beautiful to keep.

### Our belief

Growing up should not mean giving up play, creativity, or curiosity.

## Logo use

- Keep the stamp, Benlarky name, and yellow category strip together as one complete logo.
- Keep the original proportions and colors. Do not stretch, rotate, redraw, or retype the logo.
- Keep the category strip attached, its text on one line, and its width equal to the visible logo group.
- Leave clear space of at least **one quarter of the stamp’s height** around the whole logo. These standard files already include that space; do not crop it away.
- Check that both Benlarky and the category line remain readable at the final display size.
- Use the matching light- or dark-background version and keep the logo away from busy backgrounds.
